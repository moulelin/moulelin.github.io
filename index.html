About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Trinity College Dublin and a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow, currently a visiting researcher at Imperial College London hosted by Dr. Yingzhen Li.

My research focuses on probabilistic modelling and uncertainty estimation for large language models, with a particular interest in making LLMs know what they don't know. Recent work covers probabilistic low-rank adaptation of LLMs, scalable Gaussian processes, weight sharing in Bayesian neural networks, and knowledge distillation for LLM reasoning and code. At Imperial I work on stochastic processes for LLMs, and as part of my MSCA secondment with Ubotica I deploy uncertainty-aware models on satellites.

Before Trinity, I studied Artificial Intelligence at Eötvös Loránd University (Budapest) and Computer Science at Northeast Forestry University, and worked on model acceleration at Ericsson and on autonomous-driving perception at Zeekr.
